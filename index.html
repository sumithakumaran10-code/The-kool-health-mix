✦ How to Prepare

Ready in Minutes

🥄
Measure

Take 2–3 tablespoons of Satthu Maavu powder in a cup or glass.

💧
Mix

Add warm water or milk. Stir well until smooth with no lumps.

🍯
Sweeten

Add jaggery, honey or sugar to taste. A pinch of cardamom elevates the flavour.

✨
Enjoy

Serve warm or chilled. Best consumed as breakfast or an evening snack.
